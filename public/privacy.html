Vora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Vora ("the app") is a client application for connecting to a self-hosted Vora Media Server. This policy explains what information the app handles on your device and when you sign in to a server.

In short: Vora shows no ads, includes no analytics or tracking, and does not send your personal data to the app's developer or to any third-party service. The app communicates only with the media server you choose to connect to.

Information the app handles

  • Server connection details — the address of the Vora Media Server you connect to, stored locally on your device.
  • Login credentials — the username and password you enter are sent directly to the server you choose, in order to sign you in. The app then stores a login token locally on your device (encrypted) so you stay signed in. Credentials and tokens are never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • On-device preferences — settings such as your selected theme and playback options are stored locally on your device.

Information stored on your media server (not by us)

Your library, profiles, watch history, favorites, playlists, and similar data are stored on the Vora Media Server that you or your administrator operate. That server is controlled by you or your administrator, not by the developer of this app. Please refer to your server's operator for how that data is handled.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software, and does not sell or share your information with third parties.

Device permissions

  • Internet — to connect to your media server.
  • Foreground service / media playback — to play audio and video and show playback controls.
  • Notifications — to show playback status.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by the app (server address, login token, and preferences) is removed when you sign out of the app or uninstall it. Account and library data stored on your media server are managed by you or your server administrator and can be deleted on the server. For any data-related request, contact support@getvora.net.

Children

Vora is not directed at children. The app offers optional parental controls — PIN-protected profiles, content restrictions, and scheduled access — that a parent or administrator can configure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
