SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
One app for your whole media server: movies, TV, music, live TV & more.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Vora is the companion app for the self-hosted Vora Media Server. Sign in to your server and enjoy your whole collection — movies, TV shows, music, live TV, podcasts, and internet radio — on your phone, tablet, and Android TV.

Built for every screen, Vora is fast and easy to navigate, whether you're on the couch with a remote or on your phone: full-screen artwork, Continue Watching, collections, and personalized recommendations.

MOVIES & TV
- Browse your library with rich detail pages, collections, and watchlists
- Pick up right where you left off with Continue Watching
- Discover more with recommendations, cast pages, and a release calendar

MUSIC
- A full music player with synced lyrics
- Playlists and smart playlists
- Internet radio and podcasts
- Android Auto support

LIVE TV
- Live TV with a full program guide
- Favorite channels and recordings (IPTV)

FOR THE WHOLE HOUSEHOLD
- Multiple profiles, each with its own libraries and recommendations
- PIN-protected profiles, content restrictions, and scheduled access times for kids
- Multiple color themes to match your style

Vora requires access to a Vora Media Server. Run your own server or connect to one shared with you. Learn more at https://getvora.net.
